# Disguise logo for test patterns (plugin only)

The Designer plugin puts the official Disguise logo in the centre of its test
patterns, above the screen name, on black. `disguise-logo-white.svg` is the landscape
logo from the official Disguise 2023 logo pack (brand.disguise.one,
`Vectors/Disguise_Logo_Landscape.svg`) in its approved white colour; the
artwork and its built-in clear space are unchanged. If the file is missing,
test patterns are signed with DisplayForge only.

Brand rules applied (brand.disguise.one): official artwork in white, directly
on a black strip (no other plate), not outlined, shadowed, rotated or distorted, visible logo at least
15 px high, official clear space kept as the black margin.
